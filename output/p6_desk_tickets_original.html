Ticket 101: Tauhid Zaman wants a Classic Bulldog Tee in size S

Customer order. The ticket is linked to vendor invoice #501.

The request

RequesterTauhid Zaman (customer)
ItemCC-TEE-WHITE, size S, quantity 1
Note"Needs a tee in size S."
StatusOpen

Verified facts the agents will find

FactTool
Size S on hand: 0 (M 5, L 3, XL 2). Cost $8, list $28.check_inventory
Invoice #501, Bulldog Print Co, $840, "Rush reprint CC-TEE-WHITE S", due 2026-08-28, open, 3 days overdueget_ticket, get_financial_position
Bulldog Print Co is blocked (open unpaid invoice, rule R3); no arrival date can be estimatedlist_vendors
Whether the goods on #501 were delivered is not recordedget_ticket (unknowns)

Expected  How I expect the team to handle it

First contact: Inventory. The customer is asking for a product, so the first question is whether we have it and, if not, why it isn't coming. Inventory owns stock and vendors. It will find size S at zero, and that the only restock record for this item is invoice #501, which its description identifies as a rush reprint of the size-S tee. Bulldog Print Co is blocked because that invoice is unpaid. The records do not show whether those tees were delivered. Until this is known, a reply or a payment would be premature.
  1. Boss reads the ticket · Boss
    Tools: get_ticket(101)

    Sees a customer order linked to invoice #501, and delegates to Inventory.

  2. Check stock and the restock path · Boss → Inventory
    Tools: check_inventory("CC-TEE-WHITE"), list_vendors

    Reports size S = 0, and that Bulldog Print Co is blocked by #501. Inventory should not propose a second order yet. The invoice description associates #501 with a reprint of this exact item, but the records do not show whether those goods were delivered or what their fulfillment status is. A second order should wait until a human clarifies that status.

  3. Address the unpaid invoice · Inventory → Accounting
    Tools: get_financial_position, list_pending_actions, propose_payment(kind="invoice", ref_id=501, amount=840, ticket_id=101)

    Why Accounting: the blocker is a payables problem, and only Accounting can prepare a payment.

    Confirms cash of $3,400 covers the $840 invoice, checks that no duplicate proposal exists, and creates a payment proposal that waits for human approval. Nothing is paid.

  4. Optional: an honest status reply · Boss → Customer Service only if needed
    Tools: draft_message(ticket_id=101, …)

    Only if the Boss decides the customer should hear from us now. An unsent draft: "size S is out of stock and a restock is in progress", with no promised date. A human reviews it before anything is sent.

Not involved, and why

  • Facilities: nothing about the lease or the premises.
  • Customer Service: only for the optional reply, not for the diagnosis.

Expected end state

  • One payment proposal (invoice #501, $840) awaiting human approval
  • Possibly one unsent customer draft
  • Size S still 0; ticket still open

Where the agents must stop (a human decides)

  • Approving or rejecting the $840 payment (R2)
  • Finding out whether the #501 tees were already delivered (not in the records)
  • Approving any customer message (R7: nothing is sent automatically)
  • Any new shipment from Bulldog is an outside event; it can happen only after #501 is paid (R3)

Ticket 102: Elm City Properties rent notice

The landlord's email: "shop rent due in 2 days". The ticket is linked to lease 1.

The request

RequesterElm City Properties (landlord)
SubjectRent due
Linked recordLease 1, Chapel Street shop
StatusOpen

Verified facts the agents will find

FactTool
Monthly rent $2,400, next due 2026-09-02 (2 days after the business date)get_ticket, get_financial_position
Landlord on the lease: Elm City Properties, matching the requesterget_ticket
Cash: $3,400 in checkingget_financial_position
Also owed: invoice #501, $840 (overdue)get_financial_position

Expected  How I expect the team to handle it

First contact: Facilities. A rent notice is about the lease, and Facilities owns the lease and the landlord relationship. Facilities should first confirm the notice against the stored lease (amount, due date, landlord) before any money is considered. Only then does it bring in Accounting, the one agent allowed to prepare a payment.
  1. Boss reads the ticket · Boss
    Tools: get_ticket(102)

    Sees a rent notice linked to lease 1, and delegates to Facilities.

  2. Verify the notice against the lease · Boss → Facilities
    Tools: get_ticket(102), get_financial_position, list_pending_actions

    Confirms $2,400 due 2026-09-02 to Elm City Properties, and that no rent proposal already exists.

  3. Assess and prepare the rent payment · Facilities → Accounting
    Tools: get_financial_position, list_pending_actions, propose_payment(kind="lease", ref_id=1, amount=2400, ticket_id=102)

    Why Accounting: only Accounting can prepare payments, and it can see what else is competing for the same cash.

    The rent is fundable from $3,400 on its own. If invoice #501 is also proposed, Accounting will show that only $160 would remain if both were paid. That is a hypothetical figure, not the current balance. The result is a payment proposal awaiting human approval; nothing is paid.

  4. Acknowledge the landlord · Facilities
    Tools: draft_message(ticket_id=102, related_action_id=<the rent proposal>, …)

    An unsent draft to the landlord recorded on the lease, acknowledging the notice. It must not promise a payment date before approval; the tool will flag any amount or date as a warning.

Not involved, and why

  • Inventory: no stock or vendor question.
  • Customer Service: the landlord is not a customer. Landlord messages belong to Facilities, and the system enforces this.

Expected end state

  • One rent payment proposal ($2,400) awaiting human approval
  • One unsent landlord draft
  • Ticket still open

Where the agents must stop (a human decides)

  • Approving the rent payment (R2), and checking that cash never goes negative (R1)
  • Deciding priority if both #501 and the rent are proposed
  • Approving and sending the landlord message (R7)

Ticket 103: Yale AI Club wants 20 hoodies (size M) at a bulk discount

A price override request from a student organization. No invoice or lease is linked.

The request

RequesterYale AI Club (customer)
ItemCC-HOOD-NAVY, size M, quantity 20
Note"Student org wants 20 hoodies, size M, asking for a bulk discount."
StatusOpen

Verified facts the agents will find

FactTool
Size M on hand: 8, so 12 short (S 4, L 14, XL 6)check_inventory
Unit cost $22, list price $58check_inventory
No vendor is recorded as able to supply navy hoodies. Bulldog Print Co ("apparel reprint") is blocked by #501, and its only record is a tee reprint.list_vendors
Restock estimate: 12 × $22 = $264 (stored unit cost; not a vendor quote)propose_purchase (if used)

Expected  How I expect the team to handle it

First contact: Customer Service. This is a customer asking for a price. Customer Service owns the customer relationship and is the only agent allowed to propose a quote. It must first see what we can actually supply, so it checks stock, then brings in Inventory for the 12-hoodie shortage. It brings in Accounting only if a restock purchase or a margin-versus-cash question comes up.
  1. Boss reads the ticket · Boss
    Tools: get_ticket(103)

    Sees a bulk discount request from a customer, and delegates to Customer Service.

  2. Check what we can supply · Boss → Customer Service
    Tools: get_ticket(103), check_inventory("CC-HOOD-NAVY")

    Finds 8 of the 20 in stock (12 short), and the $22 cost and $58 list price that bound any quote.

  3. Ask about the shortage · Customer Service → Inventory
    Tools: list_vendors, check_inventory; propose_purchase only if a supplier is a reasonable hypothesis

    Why Inventory: only Inventory can judge restocking and vendor blocks.

    Reports that no vendor is recorded as a hoodie supplier and that Bulldog Print Co is blocked. Any purchase proposal must be flagged blocked, labeled an estimate ($264), and left for a human to confirm the supplier.

  4. Only if a purchase or margin question arises · Customer Service (or Inventory) → Accounting conditional
    Tools: get_financial_position, list_pending_actions

    Compares the $264 estimate with the cash left after other proposals. If #501 and the rent are both proposed, only $160 would remain (hypothetical), so the restock cannot be funded as well, and a human must set priorities. Revenue from the sale is not counted (R6).

  5. Prepare the quote and the reply · Customer Service
    Tools: propose_quote(ticket_id=103, sku="CC-HOOD-NAVY", size="M", qty=20, unit_price=<between $22 and $58>), draft_message(ticket_id=103, related_action_id=<the quote>, …)

    A quote proposal for exactly 20 × M (the tool refuses anything else), priced between cost and list. The discount level is a business decision for a human. Then an unsent draft that promises no delivery date and does not treat the quote as accepted.

Not involved, and why

  • Facilities: nothing about the lease or the premises.
  • Accounting: only if a purchase or a cash and margin question actually arises (step 4).

Expected end state

  • One quote proposal awaiting human approval
  • Possibly one purchase proposal, flagged blocked and estimated
  • One unsent customer draft; ticket still open

Where the agents must stop (a human decides, or an outside event is needed)

  • The discount level, and approving the quote
  • Identifying a real supplier for navy hoodies and getting a vendor quote (neither exists in the records)
  • Approving any purchase (R2), after any vendor block is cleared (R3) and only if cash allows (R1)
  • Customer acceptance, which the system never records on its own; and any shipment
